Gem Placement Meaning
Forehead The forehead gem is linked to the brain, representing high intellect, logic, planning, and a tendency to overthink
Chest The chest gem, which is placed directly over the heart, symbolizes strong emotions, passion, impulsivity, and vulnerability
Naval The naval gem represents life, creation, maternal instincts, deep empathy, and a bridge between parent and child
Hands The hand gems are associated with being hands-on, action-oriented, and direct expression of intent or relationships
Back The back gem is located just below the neck, symbolizing the weight of past trauma, fear, carrying a burden, and "looking back"
Nose The nose gem symbolizes a "hard-nosed," stubborn, and fiercely headstrong personality

This tribute will explain the lore of Steven Universe.

It starts off eons ago with the four diamonds, White, Yellow, Blue, and Pink diamond all establishing authority on a planet called, "Homeworld". Homeworld would soon be used to make other gems with their resources on the planet until they run out. The planet is stil inhabitable, but theres no more resources to make any more gems on it, so White, Yellow, and Blue diamond all start colonizing and making gems on other planets with their resources despite the fact that it is ruining the planets. They didnt let Pink diamond have a colony of her own because she was deemed too childish for a colony. About 250 years later, Pink is finaly given her own colony...

EARTH

When Pink diamond arrived on earth, she saw the beauty of it and all of the life. She assumed the alias of Rose quartz and fakes her own shattering as pink diamond. For over 250 years, Rose quartz makes up an army called the Crystal Gems. They protect the earth from. The main crystal gems included, Rose quartz, originally pink diamond, Pearl, a defective pearl who followed Pink diamonds every order, including being the one to plot her fake shattering and then live on earth, Garnet, who is a fusion between a Ruby and a Sapphire, which fusion on homeworld is high frowned upon, especially between a low-class ruby and a very high-class sapphire, and a Bismuth who didnt want to be under homeworlds rule any longer.

The Homeworld and Earth war takes place and a bunch of gems on either side are shattered, the battlefield lies full of swords and hammers and axes. The diamonds finally have enough and decide to use their combined power to blast and destroy the planet. Rose shields the blast so it doesnt hurt any humans or the earth. The blast still corrupts almost every single gem on Earth.

500 years later, Amethyst emerges from the kindergarten, which is a place where amethysts are mass produced, being the only one to emerge 500 years later than she was supposed to. She is found by the Crystal gems and is taken in.

Roughly 3950 years pass and a person named Greg comes to Beach city. He plays a concert there where the only person to show up, is Rose quartz. They eventually get together.

16 years later, Rose quartz gives up her physical form to birth her son, Steven, who is a half-gem half-human hybrid.

PRESENT DAY

Steven is 14 at this time. He lives with Pearl, Garnet, and Amethyst in the temple on Beach City. The first lore in the show starts when Steven finds a pink lion that was supposedly Rose quartz's pet. The lion has a portal in its mane that leads to a secret room where the floor is fur. There is a single island in the middle of it with a tree, a chest, and more stuff. He can also not breathe while in lions mane. In this room, Steven finds a tape recorder that has a message from his mother, Rose quartz. She tells him that he is special and that he is the only one who can save the earth from Homeworld.

Nothing else too interesting happens, until later on in the show when steven is given a mirror from pearl because he asked her to help him learn like hes in school. The mirror apparently knows everything, but it doesnt do anything. When steven takes the mirror with him alone, the mirror starts talking to him with phrases that he has said before. Eventually, the mirror tells him to free the gem on the mirrors back by taking it out. He does so, and out pops lapis. The Crystal gems try to attack Lapis, so she flees, take the ocean and creating a pillar to get off of Earth. Steven goes up to her with the help of her moving him through the water pillar. When he arrives at the top where lapis is, he finds that her gem is cracked, making her inable to just fly off the planet with her hydrokinesis. Steven licks his hand, and then puts it on her gem, and it gets healed. Its the power of Rose Quartz that he hasn't been able to do on command until now. She flies away and returns the ocean.

The next lore is when Steven is given a mission to go to the moon base of Homeworld. He goes there and finds a bunch of corrupted gems, and a gem called Peridot. She is a technician for Homeworld, and she is trying to fix the communication system on the moon base. She is also trying to get back to Homeworld. Steven tries to stop her from leaving, but she refuses. She eventually gets captured by the Crystal Gems, and they take her back to the temple. They try to convince her to stay on Earth, but she refuses. Eventually, she starts to like Earth and the Crystal Gems, and she decides to stay.

Next is when the cluster is revealed. The cluster is over a million tiny gem shards combined into one huge planet destroying cluster that is resting in the core of the earth waiting to form and destroy it. Before it is about to emerge, Peridot and Steven build a drill and drill down to it. They hope to destroy it with the drill but that ends up not working and so Steven passes out. He wakes up, but instead, he wakes up in the clusters mind. He speaks to the cluster and convinces it to bubble itself so that it doesnt form and destroy the Earth.

Next, the diamonds come to Earth. They are mad at the Crystal Gems for protecting the Earth and not letting them take it over. They try to convince Steven to give them the Earth, but he refuses. They eventually leave, but not before giving Steven a warning that they will be back.

Eventually, they do return. They fight the Crystal gems and after a bunch of fighting, the Diamonds find out that Steven is technically Rose quartz who was Pink diamond. They ally together and the Diamonds try to get Steven to Live with them because he reminds them so much of Pink diamond who they've been mourning for a while. Steven obviously declines, but decides to try to talk to White diamond who has been in her part of the ship nad hasn't gone out since Pink diamond was supposably shattered. White obviously doesn't listen, and instead takes control of Garnet, Pearl, and Amethyst, and then seperates Stevens gem from his body. After that, Stevens gem and Steven come back together after knocking out white. After she wakes up, Steven finally gets through to her and brings her back to her former self.

After that, Steven and the Crystal Gems are finally able to live in peace with the Diamonds. Steven is finally able to live a normal life with his friends and family.

THE MOVIE

The movie starts out with everything being normal. Then out of nowhere, a giant injector flies from the sky, and digs into the Earth, injecting a purple ooze that seems to be killing the Earth around it.

Steven and the Crystal Gems try to stop it, but they are too late. The injector has already injected the ooze into the Earth, and it is starting to spread. Steven tries to use his powers to stop it, but he is unable to. The Crystal Gems try to help him, but they are also unable to stop it as well.

Eventually, Steven and the Crystal Gems find out that the injector is being controlled by a new gem called Spinel. She was Pink diamonds playmate, but she was abandoned by her when she was supposed to come back after checking out her colony. She is mad at Steven for being Pink diamond, and she wants to destroy the Earth as revenge for Pink diamond leaving her in the garden for thousands of years.

Spinel then uses something called a rejuvenator which basically reverts gems back to their original state. He hits all of them, but Steven doesnt get hurt, his powers only revert back to how they were when he first got them. Steven uses the rejuvenator on spinel and reverts her to back before she got left in the garden by Pink diamond.

Steven then finds out that if he wants to revert the Crystal gems back to their original states, he must treat them like a puzzle. Give them all their pieces, and they will return back to normal.

Steven then does this, and the Crystal gems return back to normal, but not after they fight Spinel again because she thought Steven was only using her to turn off the injector and then he would rejuvenate her. After that is solved, everything goes back to normal, and Spinel goes with the diamonds back to homeworld because Spinel also reminds the diamnds of Pink.
