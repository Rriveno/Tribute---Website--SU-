Gem Placement Meaning
Forehead The forehead gem is linked to the brain, representing high intellect, logic, planning, and a tendency to overthink
Chest The chest gem, which is placed directly over the heart, symbolizes strong emotions, passion, impulsivity, and vulnerability
Naval The naval gem represents life, creation, maternal instincts, deep empathy, and a bridge between parent and child
Hands The hand gems are associated with being hands-on, action-oriented, and direct expression of intent or relationships
Back The back gem is located just below the neck, symbolizing the weight of past trauma, fear, carrying a burden, and "looking back"
Nose The nose gem symbolizes a "hard-nosed," stubborn, and fiercely headstrong personality

This tribute will explain the lore of Steven Universe.

It starts off eons ago with the four diamonds, White, Yellow, Blue, and Pink diamond all establishing authority on a planet called, "Homeworld". Homeworld would soon be used to make other gems with their resources on the planet until they run out. The planet is stil inhabitable, but theres no more resources to make any more gems on it, so White, Yellow, and Blue diamond all start colonizing and making gems on other planets with their resources despite the fact that it is ruining the planets. They didnt let Pink diamond have a colony of her own because she was deemed too childish for a colony. About 250 years later, Pink is finaly given her own colony...

EARTH

When Pink diamond arrived on earth, she saw the beauty of it and all of the life. She assumed the alias of Rose quartz and fakes her own shattering as pink diamond. For over 250 years, Rose quartz makes up an army called the Crystal Gems. They protect the earth from. The main crystal gems included, Rose quartz, originally pink diamond, Pearl, a defective pearl who followed Pink diamonds every order, including being the one to plot her fake shattering and then live on earth, Garnet, who is a fusion between a Ruby and a Sapphire, which fusion on homeworld is high frowned upon, especially between a low-class ruby and a very high-class sapphire, and a Bismuth who didnt want to be under homeworlds rule any longer.

The Homeworld and Earth war takes place and a bunch of gems on either side are shattered, the battlefield lies full of swords and hammers and axes. The diamonds finally have enough and decide to use their combined power to blast and destroy the planet. Rose shields the blast so it doesnt hurt any humans or the earth. The blast still corrupts almost every single gem on Earth.

500 years later, Amethyst emerges from the kindergarten, which is a place where amethysts are mass produced, being the only one to emerge 500 years later than she was supposed to. She is found by the Crystal gems and is taken in.

Roughly 3950 years pass and a person named Greg comes to Beach city. He plays a concert there where the only person to show up, is Rose quartz. They eventually get together.

16 years later, Rose quartz gives up her physical form to birth her son, Steven, who is a half-gem half-human hybrid.
