Gem Placement Meaning
Forehead The forehead gem is linked to the brain, representing high intellect, logic, planning, and a tendency to overthink
Chest The chest gem, which is placed directly over the heart, symbolizes strong emotions, passion, impulsivity, and vulnerability
Naval The naval gem represents life, creation, maternal instincts, deep empathy, and a bridge between parent and child
Hands The hand gems are associated with being hands-on, action-oriented, and direct expression of intent or relationships
Back The back gem is located just below the neck, symbolizing the weight of past trauma, fear, carrying a burden, and "looking back"
Nose The nose gem symbolizes a "hard-nosed," stubborn, and fiercely headstrong personality
