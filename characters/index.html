Steven Universe is a kind-hearted, cheerful, and empathetic boy who is half-human and half-Gem. As the son of Rose Quartz, he inherits incredible powers and joins the Crystal Gems to protect Earth. Steven believes in understanding others, solving problems peacefully, and giving everyone a chance to change. Despite facing difficult challenges, he stays hopeful and uses his compassion, courage, and determination to help others and discover who he truly is.

Pearl from Steven Universe is a graceful, intelligent, and disciplined Crystal Gem who serves as one of Steven’s guardians and mentors. Originally a servant to Pink Diamond, Pearl struggles with her past, her unrequited love for Rose Quartz, and her desire to feel useful and needed. She is perfectionistic, protective, and sometimes overbearing, but beneath her elegant and composed exterior lies deep insecurity and emotional vulnerability. Throughout the series, Pearl learns to let go of the past, embrace her independence, and discover her own identity beyond her devotion to others.

Garnet from Steven Universe is a strong, calm, and dependable Crystal Gem who serves as a leader and protector for Steven. She is the fusion of Ruby and Sapphire, representing the power of love, trust, and commitment. Garnet is confident and level-headed, often offering wise advice while remaining composed in difficult situations. Although she can seem mysterious and reserved, she deeply cares for her friends and values individuality, emotional honesty, and the freedom to choose one’s own future.

Amethyst from Steven Universe is a fun-loving, rebellious, and energetic Crystal Gem who brings humor and spontaneity to the group. She enjoys joking around, shapeshifting, and breaking the rules, often acting more carefree and immature than the other Gems. However, beneath her playful exterior, Amethyst struggles with insecurity and feelings of inadequacy because she was created differently from other Gems. Throughout the series, she learns to accept herself, embrace her uniqueness, and recognize that her worth isn’t defined by how she compares to others.
